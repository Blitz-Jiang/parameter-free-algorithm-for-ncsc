\begin{abstract}
We study fully parameter-free second-order methods for smooth
nonconvex--strongly-concave minimax optimization. Our analysis uses an
existing implicit gradient estimator and its known quadratic accuracy
with respect to the inner solution error. Combining this oracle with
an inexact universal trust-region framework and a cumulative cubic
path analysis, we first obtain a known-parameter method with leading
inner-gradient complexity
$O((1+\log\kappa)\Delta_P\kappa^2\sqrt{\rho}\,\epsilon^{-3/2})$.
This removes the multiplicative $\log(1/\epsilon)$ factor in the
explicit inner-gradient bound of Minimax Cubic Newton.
We then develop a fully parameter-free method based on
gradient-scaled trust regions and persistent SCAR tracking.
To reach an $O(\epsilon,\sqrt{\kappa^3\rho\epsilon})$ second-order
stationary point of the primal function $P(x)=\max_y f(x,y)$,
its leading trust-region subproblem and inner-gradient complexities
are
$O(\Delta_P\kappa^{3/2}\sqrt{\rho}\,\epsilon^{-3/2})$ and
$O(\Delta_P\kappa^2\sqrt{\rho}\,\epsilon^{-3/2})$, respectively,
with additive instance-dependent polylogarithmic terms in
$1/\epsilon$.
Here $\Delta_P$ is the initial primal-function gap, $\kappa$ the
condition number, and $\rho$ the Hessian-Lipschitz constant of $f$.
The parameter-free guarantees hold for sufficiently small $\epsilon$,
with an unknown threshold depending only on the regularity constants
and uniform over admissible objectives and initializations.
\end{abstract}
