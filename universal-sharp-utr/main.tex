\begin{abstract}
We analyze a curvature-scaled universal trust-region method for
unconstrained nonconvex optimization under the generalized smoothness
condition $\norm{\nabla^3 f(x)}\le L_0+L_1\norm{\nabla f(x)}$.
The method uses exact second-order information and globally solved
quadratic trust-region subproblems without knowledge of $L_0$ or $L_1$.
For independently specified gradient and curvature tolerances, it
attains the universal sharp four-term oracle-complexity bound, with
only accuracy-independent additive logarithms. A joint scale--gradient
potential controls both interior and boundary steps. We give the
algorithm, the global convergence theorem, and a self-contained proof.
\end{abstract}
