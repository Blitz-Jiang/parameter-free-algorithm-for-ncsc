\section{Problem setting and assumptions}
\label{sec:setting}
We consider $\min_{x\in\R^n}f(x)$ under the generalized smoothness
condition of \citet[Assumption~1]{fang2026parameter}.

\begin{assumption}[Generalized smoothness]\label{ass:setting}
The function $f\in C^3(\R^n)$ is bounded below:
$f_*:=\inf_{x\in\R^n}f(x)>-\infty$. There exist constants
$L_0>0$ and $L_1\ge0$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:assumption}
 \norm{\nabla^3 f(x)}\le L_0+L_1\norm{\nabla f(x)}
 \qquad\text{for every }x\in\R^n.
\end{equation}
\end{assumption}

Let $\Delta:=f(x_0)-f_*$. Vector norms are Euclidean and tensor
norms are induced operator norms. For independent tolerances
$\epsilon,\delta>0$, an $(\epsilon,\delta)$-second-order stationary
point satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stationarity}
 \norm{\nabla f(x)}\le\epsilon,\qquad
 \lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 f(x))\ge-\delta.
\end{equation}
Write $[z]_+:=\max\{z,0\}$ and
$\lp t:=\max\{\log t,0\}$ for $t>0$, with $\lp0:=0$.
The algorithm below uses quadratic trust-region subproblems and
observable acceptance tests, without knowledge of $L_0$ or $L_1$.
